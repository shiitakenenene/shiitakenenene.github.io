sponsorship
======
my researches (e.g. math) can take a long time to be serious or mature enough to be published. before that, I cannot really expect any paybacks on them. besides, once your works have been published, it is usually other people who decide how much you should earn for them. this completely goes against my initial wishes: my researches are only motivated by my personal emotions-- loneliness, sadness, happiness and etc.-- to make my days meaningful, and I don't want to care about how others would assess my work.

however, I still need some money to live in this world. so I would appreciate it if you are willing to kindly sponsor my researches and my life. I may spend the money on a coffee, a lunch, a book, or supporting other artists I love (they are great! you can also check out my wishlists below). every dollar is meaningful and will be highly appreciated.

I may post a few drafts of my immature work on the website, so you can check if you're interested or if that would be a good reason to sponsor me. currently you can sponsor me at [afdian](https://afdian.com/a/shiitakenenene) (support WeChat and Alipay).

huge THANKS for reading this and have a nice day :)
